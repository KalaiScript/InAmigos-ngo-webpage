About InAmigos

Building a brighter future through collective action and community empowerment.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, InAmigos started with a simple belief: that small acts of kindness can spark massive change. What began as a local student initiative has grown into a structured NGO dedicated to uplifting underprivileged communities.

We work on the front lines of education, healthcare, and environmental sustainability, ensuring that every project creates a lasting impact on the ground.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: We lead with empathy in every initiative.
  • Transparency: Openness in all our operations and fund usage.
  • Sustainability: Focusing on long-term growth rather than short-term fixes.
